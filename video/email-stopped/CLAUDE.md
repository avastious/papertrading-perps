## Files
- `lib/puppet.js`: the puppet rig. `renderCharacter(spec, {pose, face, look, flip, x, y, scale, shadow})` returns an SVG `<g>` with the feet on y=0. It works in Node and in the browser (`globalThis.Puppet`).
  - Poses: `stand, walk, run, point, shrug, surprised`. You can also pass a custom pose object. `walkPose(phase)` and `runPose(phase)` give continuous cycles for animation (phase in turns).
  - Faces: `neutral, happy, angry, scared, surprised, blink, talkA, talkB`.
  - `walkPose`/`runPose` were fixed on 28 Nov: the planted leg used to swing forward (moonwalk).
  - Angle convention: 0 = down, +90 = forward (+x), 180 = up. Limbs are `[upper, lower]` absolute angles. L = far side (drawn behind), R = near side.
  - Swappable parts: `HAIR`, `ACCESSORY`, `HAT`, `OUTFIT`, `PROPS` tables.
- `lib/cast.js`: specs for Penny Park (lead), Gate (the front-door server dressed as a doorman), Sam (on-call engineer), Mr Bigsby (bank trader) and Faxie (a 1998 fax machine).
- `tools/sheets.js`: renders `out/step1/cast_sheet.png` and `out/step1/penny_poses.png` with headless Chrome. Run it with `node tools/sheets.js`; set `CHROME_PATH` if Chrome isn't found.

## Rules
- Original characters only. No real people, no caricatures of executives, no Microsoft or other company logos (use a generic envelope/mail icon).
- Style: flat 2D, big heads, simple faces, thick ink outlines (`#1d1a24`, 6 px).
- Keep every version. Write new renders to new folders (`out/step4/v2/`, ...); never overwrite old ones.

## Story facts (checked 28 Nov 2026; flag anything unverified in the script; the sources list in `script/beats.py` is the fuller, newer version)
- The outage began on Thu 12 Nov 2026, around 16:20–17:54 UTC; Outlook complaints surged around 12:25 pm ET. Reports came from 74 countries and territories within about an hour.
- Outlook and Exchange Online email were down for more than a day. Teams, SharePoint and Copilot were also disrupted. Full recovery came on 14 Nov (about 36 hours by provider timestamps).
- Banks, governments and courts were hit. Finance firms in Taiwan and Japan confirmed trades by fax (Reuters, Bloomberg); another Taiwanese firm cancelled a bond repurchase.
- Microsoft said it was not a cyberattack. Its preliminary cause was a latent defect in the networking gateway ("front door") servers that front Exchange Online. The defect was introduced during an earlier network capacity upgrade and exposed by a later network change, with a surge in load as a secondary factor ("three factors"). Exchange Online can't route around that gateway.
- Sources: Al Jazeera (13 Nov), Office Watch, ITPro, ABC News (16 Nov), Crypto Briefing.
