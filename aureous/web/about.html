Why it exists

Perpetual futures are easy to start and easy to get wrong. Leverage, funding and liquidation behave in ways that only make sense once you've watched them happen to your own positions. Aureous lets you learn that with real market conditions and fake money, so the expensive lessons cost nothing.

Where the numbers come from

Every price, order book, chart and funding rate is read live from Hyperliquid's public market data. When you place an order, it's filled against the real order book at that moment, so a large order pays the same slippage it would on the real exchange. Nothing is sent to Hyperliquid, and no real orders are ever placed.

What's simulated

  • Market and limit orders, including post-only, immediate-or-cancel and reduce-only.
  • Take-profit and stop-loss orders, triggered on the mark price.
  • Cross margin and leverage up to each market's maximum.
  • Trading fees at Hyperliquid's base rates: 0.045% when you take liquidity, 0.015% when you provide it.
  • Hourly funding using Hyperliquid's actual funding history.
  • Liquidation when your equity falls below maintenance margin.

The details, and the few places the simulation is simpler than the real thing, are on How it works.

The leaderboard

Everyone starts with the same $10,000, and the leaderboard ranks accounts by their current value, including open positions at the mark price. Trades are checked on the Aureous server against live prices, so a balance can't be edited to climb the rankings.

You'll also find 20 Aureous bots on the board, each tagged BOT. Each one trades a simple strategy (trend following, breakouts, mean reversion, buying dips, a maximum-leverage degen) on the same live prices, fees and rules as you. Click one to see its strategy and trades, then try to beat it.

Click any trader to see their open positions and recent trades, and learn from the best on the board. Account-value milestones earn Roman-coin badges, from the bronze Sestertius at $20,000 to Imperium at $1,000,000. Each new badge also earns a week in the spotlight.

Who's behind it

Aureous is an independent project. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Hyperliquid. The name comes from the Latin aureus, golden, and the gold coin of ancient Rome.
